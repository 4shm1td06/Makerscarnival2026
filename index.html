// About the Event

Where Makers
Come Alive

Makers Carnival is the annual flagship event of JU Makerspace — North India's first student-driven collaborative cell, housed at JECRC University, Jaipur. This isn't just an event. It's a movement.

Every edition brings together passionate builders, tinkerers, and innovators under one roof to showcase bold ideas — from humanoid robots and lie detection systems to immersive mood lighting and autonomous vehicles.

Through interactive workshops, live competitions, and a culture of open collaboration, Makers Carnival ignites the spark that turns curious students into confident creators.

// What to Expect

Five Arenas
of Making

01 🤖

Project Exhibitions

The heart of the carnival. Students display their most ambitious builds — humanoid robots, lie detectors, mood-adaptive lighting systems, and technologies that push the boundaries of what's possible in a university lab.

Main Stage
02 🚁

Drone & Dart Car Showcase

Dedicated tech displays for aerial drones and ground-based dart cars. Watch precision engineering in action, live on the floor.

Tech Arena
03 🥽

AR/VR & 3D Printing Workshops

Get your hands dirty. Skill-building sessions in emerging technologies — augmented reality, virtual reality, and additive manufacturing for beginners and enthusiasts alike.

Workshop Zone
04 ⚔️

Hackathon & Battlefield

Competitive coding sprints, interactive treasure hunts, and the fan-favourite "Battlefield" floor event. Come prepared — only the sharpest minds survive.

Competition Floor
05 🎵

Jam Session Finale

Every Makers Carnival ends the way it should — with music, celebration, and the collective energy of a community that built something remarkable together. The Jam Session is where the maker spirit takes centre stage.

Evening Closing
